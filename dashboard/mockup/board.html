Goal

Pantry helps a household know what food it already has before going shopping. This workstream adds barcode scanning, so an item goes in with one tap instead of typing its name.

Done looks like

  • Scanning a packaged item adds it, with name and size, in under three seconds.
  • An unknown barcode falls back to manual entry without losing the scan.
  • The shopping list stays in sync across two phones in the same household.

Not in this workstream

Reading receipts, tracking prices, and suggesting recipes.
